Theme
Foundations / Elevation

Elevation

Four levels, as in Material 3, for things that sit above the page, plus the edges of panels and sheets. Shadows carry the depth; in dark they are stronger, and side panels gain a hairline edge.

Levels

Each level is two shadows: a tight one that defines the edge and a soft one that sets the distance. Light shadows are tinted with the darkest neutral, so they stay green-gray rather than dirty; dark shadows are pure black.

Edges

Panels, sheets and bars cast their shadow toward the page they cover. In light the shadow alone separates them; in dark a shadow barely shows, so the side panel, the top bar, the sidebar and the Edit layout bar draw a 1 px edge in a darker neutral instead of, or as well as, the shadow.

Guidelines

  • Elevation follows behavior, not importance: a card is level 1 whatever it contains.
  • A card being dragged lifts to level 4 and settles back to level 1 when dropped.
  • In the dark theme a shadow alone is hard to see, so side panels and bars also draw a 1 px edge.
  • A menu or a list opened from something keeps one level above it: menus at 2 over cards at 1, dialogs at 3.
  • Never add a shadow to show state (hover, selection): elevation changes only when something lifts, as a dragged card.
