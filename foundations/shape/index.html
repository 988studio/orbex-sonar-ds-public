Foundations / Shape

Shape

The corner scale of Material 3 Expressive. Corners grow with the container: a chip is barely rounded, a side panel is generously so, and pills and dots are fully round.

Corner scale

  • none

    0 px

    --sonar-shape-corner-none

  • extra-small

    4 px

    Tooltips, small marks.

    --sonar-shape-corner-extra-small

  • small

    8 px

    Chips, pressed buttons.

    --sonar-shape-corner-small

  • medium

    12 px

    Cards, fields, menu items.

    --sonar-shape-corner-medium

  • large

    16 px

    Menus, banners, buttons.

    --sonar-shape-corner-large

  • large-increased

    20 px

    The step between large and extra-large in the Material 3 scale. No component uses it yet.

    --sonar-shape-corner-large-increased

  • extra-large

    28 px

    Side panels, search field.

    --sonar-shape-corner-extra-large

  • full

    Full

    Pills, avatars, status dots.

    --sonar-shape-corner-full

Guidelines

  • Nested shapes take a smaller corner than their container, so the curves stay parallel.
  • A pressed button changes shape instead of shrinking: its corner goes from large to small, the Material 3 Expressive way of acknowledging a tap.
  • Only edges that float get rounded. A sheet attached to the bottom of the screen is rounded on top only.
