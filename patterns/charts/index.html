Patterns / Charts

Charts

Three shapes cover the overview: a tick bar for a share, a ring for the parts of a whole, a line for a month day by day, and one legend for all of them. They are drawn in SVG and CSS with the chart roles of the tokens; no charting library.

Tick bar

One row of fine ticks, each part in its share of the width and in its chart role. The parts meet without a gap, and a legend under the bar names each color.

Ring chart

Parts of a whole as a ring of 96 ticks, the Inventory donut. The hole can hold the total; the legend names each color.

Trend chart

A month day by day as a line, with its running total dotted against a second axis. The legend turns each series off and on; hovering, touching or the arrow keys read a day.

Rules

  • Color by role. The first series in accent, the second in the strong neutral, the rest of a whole in the rest tone, alerts in danger. Never a hue that means nothing.
  • A series that is off keeps its name readable. It drops to the tertiary level at full opacity and its marker turns hollow; it is never faded.
  • Figures in the data role, the mono face at 10 px, with tabular numerals; gridlines dotted and quiet.
  • Labels beside the chart, not inside it. The card states the key figures in text; the chart shows their shape.
  • No animation on load. A chart draws at once; values move only when the unit or the period changes.

Do and don’t

Accessibility

Keyboard

Key What it does
Tab Reaches the trend chart and its legend buttons. Tick bars and rings take no focus.
Left and Right arrows In the trend chart: read the previous or next day.
Home and End Go to the first day or the last day with data.
Escape Clears the day being read.
Enter or Space On a legend button: shows or hides that series.

Roles and attributes

Element or attribute What it gives
role="img" with aria-label Tick bars and rings: one name lists every part with its value.
role="group" The trend chart, named by its label with a hint to use the arrow keys.
aria-live="polite" The trend chart reads the chosen day: “May 12: 350 plt, 4,485 plt so far”.
aria-pressed Legend buttons say whether their series is shown.
Chart legend aria-hidden when the chart’s own name already lists the parts, as in every demo here.

Notes

  • A chart is never the only way to a figure: the card states its key figures in text beside it.
  • Color is never the only cue: a legend names every color, and a series that is off keeps its name readable.
  • The parts of one chart differ in lightness as well as hue (accent, strong and rest; or danger and rest), so they stay distinct without color vision.

Built with

Each chart is its own component, with its API: Tick bar, Ring chart, Trend chart and Chart legend. The trend chart has a budget of 4 KB instead of 3: it draws axes, two series, a legend and reading by pointer and keys.
