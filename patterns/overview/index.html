Patterns / Overview

Overview

The six cards of the default overview on the grid, live, with the figures of one day: Tuesday, May 12, 2026. Change a unit, turn a week, read a day of shipments; narrow the window and the columns follow the reading order of the desktop layout.

Live

Rules

  • One overview, arranged once. People arrange it on a desktop; tablets and phones read that arrangement in order. See Breakpoints and grid.
  • Columns end level. Card heights are chosen so that M + L = S + XL, and the default overview keeps a straight bottom edge.
  • What matters most goes first. Orders and Daily shipments open the reading order because they are what people check every morning.
  • Every card leads somewhere. The overview never becomes the place where work is done; it says where to go.
  • Units are per card. Pallets, kilograms or items are chosen in each card, because each team counts differently.
