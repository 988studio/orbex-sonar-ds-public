Patterns / Cards

Cards

The overview is made of cards. Each sums up one part of the business, a figure first and the detail after, and leads to the page where the work is done. They are built only from the system’s components and tokens: Card, Card link, Card scroll and Unit menu, with the charts.

Anatomy

Orders

Open total3,940 plt

Rows that scroll first
Go to Orders
123456
  1. Title, which also names the card as a region of the page.
  2. Unit menu: the card’s only setting, the unit its figures are shown in. Changing it converts every figure in the card. See Unit menu.
  3. Summary: one figure that sums the card up, a label and a value over a rule.
  4. Content: the detail, at most two levels deep: sections with a small heading, rows, a chart.
  5. Scrolling list, optional: the part of the content that gives way first when the size leaves no room. See Card scroll.
  6. Footer: the way to the full page, and at most one action, such as New order. See Card link.

In edit mode

M
12345
  1. Move handle: a pill centered on the top edge. Drag it to move the card; with the keyboard, Space picks the card up and drops it.
  2. Remove: a red ring outside the top end corner. The card leaves the overview and can be added back from New element.
  3. Dashed edge in accent: the card is part of a draft until Save layout.
  4. Resize handle: a pill centered on the bottom edge. Pull it, or use the up and down arrows, to step through the sizes the card offers.
  5. Size: S, M, L or XL, in a ring beside the resize handle.

/* ---------- Card tokens ----------
   Distances inside the cards are steps of the spacing scale. A few values are not, because a card's rhythm depends on
   them; each is named here with its reason, and nothing else in this file uses a bare distance. */
.sonar-card {
  /* Deliveries and Balance: a row of a day is 38 px between rules, one line of 14 px text, so its air is 11.5 px
     above and below. Rows of every card line up with it */
  --card-row-height: 38px;
  --card-row-line: 14px;
  --card-row-air: calc((var(--card-row-height) - var(--card-row-line) - 1px) / 2);

     and the rules stay put while the rows scroll */
  --card-day-band: 41px;

  --card-list-rhythm: 13px;

Optional cards

Five more modules can join the overview, from New element while editing or from All modules: Invoices, Credit notes, Notifications, Latest documents and Scheduled orders. They come in one size, L (405 px), and are built like the six: a title, a list that scrolls between header and footer, and a link to their page. A new module goes to the foot of the shortest column.

Sizes

In the overview a card is 245, 375, 405 or 535 px high and as wide as its column. Each card is designed for the sizes where its content reads well, and offers only those when people edit the overview. A larger size shows more of the same content, never different content.

States

At rest a card is a region with its own controls. While the overview is edited it gains a dashed edge, a handle to move it, a button to remove it and a handle to resize it; it lifts while it is dragged and leaves a drop placeholder where it will land. The live version is in Editing the overview.

  • Edit: a 1 px dashed edge in accent. The handles are pills of 36 × 16 px with an accent ring on the card’s surface; remove is a 22 px ring in danger outside the top end corner. Each is a button with a name: “Move Orders”, “Remove Orders from the overview”, “Resize Orders”.
  • Hover and focus fill the handle with accent and turn its icon to on-accent; remove fills with danger.
  • Lifted: the card rises to elevation 4 at 103 % and loses its dashed edge, and its move handle stays solid while it is held.
  • Drop placeholder: the slot the card leaves, as tall as the card, with a dashed accent edge on the accent container.
  • Resizing steps through the sizes the card offers, never in between. A label over the handle names the size and its height, and says when the columns end level.

Rules

  • A figure first. The summary says the one number someone opens the overview for; the content explains it.
  • Figures align. They use tabular numerals, and a figure keeps its unit beside it: 1,420 plt, never a bare 1,420.
  • Status in words. Rows show state with the Status component, a dot and a word, never color alone.
  • A card summarizes; the page acts. Long tasks open their panel or page; the card keeps at most one action.
  • Header and footer stay put. When content does not fit a size, it scrolls between them: a list marked with CardScroll gives way first (the deliveries of a day, the products to reorder), and the whole content scrolls only if that is not enough. A fade at the edge says there is more; a thin indicator shows while scrolling.
  • Days of another month are not shown in a card’s month grid: their cells stay empty. The week strip runs on across months, since the chosen day always sits in its middle.
  • A card that opens grows. The month view of a calendar and the payment calendar of Balance take the height they need; they fold back to the card’s size.
  • Rows open their details beside the overview. View, New order and Reorder open a side panel; the row whose details are open stays marked.

Do and don’t

Accessibility

Keyboard

Key What it does
Tab Moves through the card’s controls in reading order: unit menu, content, footer.
Unit menu Opens with Enter, Space or Down arrow, like any menu.
Up and Down arrows, Page Up and Page Down On content that scrolls, once it has focus: scroll it. It takes focus only while it scrolls.
Left and Right arrows On the chosen day of a calendar (Deliveries, Payment calendar): the previous or next day. In the month, Up and Down move a week.

Element or attribute What it gives
<section aria-labelledby> Each card is a region named by its title, so the overview can be read card by card. Where the same card shows twice, label names each copy: “Orders, size S”.
Unit menu Named with what it shows and what that means: “Unit: plt, Pallets”, so voice control can reach it by its visible text. Its choices are a group of radio items.
Summary bar The bar between label and value is drawn, so it is never read aloud.
role="group" while scrolling Content that scrolls is a group named by its list (“Deliveries of May 12”) or by the card’s title, so focus arriving on it says what it is. The fade and the indicator are drawn and hidden.

Notes

  • Buttons repeated in rows carry names that say what they act on: “View delivery 04-127C”, not “View” six times.
  • What changes when a step button is used is announced: the day of a delivery list, the due date in Balance, the month in Daily shipments.
  • The faded next day in Deliveries (XL, and the month view) is a preview: it is hidden from assistive technology and takes no focus.
  • In the week strip only the chosen day is in the tab order; the arrow keys move the day and focus follows it. Each day is named in full: “Saturday, May 16, no deliveries”.
  • Where the same card shows in several sizes, as on this page, each copy is named with its size, so no two regions share a name.
  • See Charts for the charts inside the cards.

Built with

The cards are built from Card, Card link, Card scroll and Unit menu, with Tick bar, Ring chart, Trend chart and Chart legend. Each page has its API.
